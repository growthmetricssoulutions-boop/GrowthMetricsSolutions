Growth Metrics Solutions was founded on one belief: every business decision should be backed by data — not guesswork.

Mohd Anas Khan began his career in 2019 as a professional Data Analyst. Over the years he worked with global MNCs like iEnergizer, EY, Ipsos and KPMG, where every report, every recommendation and every decision had to be proven with numbers.

Coming back to the ground reality of Indian business, he saw the opposite. Sellers on Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho were running ads without tracking returns. Shops were boosting Instagram posts without knowing if a single customer came from them. Businesses were paying for websites that never brought an enquiry.

Growth Metrics Solutions was created to bring MNC-level data thinking to everyday sellers and growing brands — one team that builds your website, manages your marketplaces and runs your Meta ads, then shows you exactly what's working.

That's what the name means: growth, measured.
